# Digital PR & Backlink Strategy

**Principle:** earn links and mentions by being genuinely useful to publications and communities. No purchased links, private blog networks, link exchanges, fake press releases or manufactured reviews. Track quality, not count.

## Assets worth pitching (all real)
1. **Case-study angles:** direct primary care site that explains a new model of care in plain language; bilingual direct-booking platform with PMS and local payments; vacation-rental site with a custom booking plugin.
2. **Expert commentary** the founder can give credibly: what patients look for before enrolling; why direct booking beats listing platforms; Webflow vs WordPress trade-offs.
3. **Original, defensible data** (only if you collect it): e.g. an anonymised review of 20 clinic websites against a checklist you publish.

## Targets (research each before contacting; see `11-backlink-tracker.csv`)
Web design/dev publications · healthcare-practice and telehealth newsletters · hospitality/vacation-rental trade media · Webflow community/showcase · founder and agency podcasts · and **your own clients' sites** (ask permission for a "Website by Ignixel Studio" credit; these are real, relevant links).

## Process
1. Pick one story per month. 2. Find 10 relevant editors/hosts; read their recent work. 3. Send a short, specific pitch (what is new, why their readers care, the proof, available assets). 4. Follow up once. 5. Log everything in the tracker. 6. Award submissions (Awwwards, CSS Design Awards) only for projects that genuinely meet the bar; fees apply (verify).

## Measure
Referring domains and their relevance · referral traffic and enquiries from each placement · brand mentions · publication authority. Review quarterly; disavow nothing unless Search Console reports a manual action.
