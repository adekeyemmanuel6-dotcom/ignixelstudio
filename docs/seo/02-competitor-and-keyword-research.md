# Competitor & Keyword Research

## What was and was not possible
This environment cannot reach Google, ignixels.com, SEO tools (Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, Keyword Planner), review sites or AI platforms. **No competitor was analysed and no search volume or difficulty figure was produced.** Nothing below is a claim about a competitor.

What *was* done: the keyword universe you specified (agency, platform, design, application/AI, industry and geographic terms) was mapped to pages and prioritised using **the strength of Ignixel's own portfolio evidence**. See `03-keyword-research.csv` (64 rows; every volume, difficulty and competition cell reads "Not measured").

## Prioritisation logic used
| Priority | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Capability proven by multiple projects and a page exists | Webflow agency, WordPress development agency, healthcare web design, direct primary care / telehealth website design, vacation rental website design |
| P2 | Capability evidenced, page supports it partially | UI/UX design agency, custom web application development |
| P3 | Evidence thin or broad head term; support with content | web design agency (head term), landing page design, local-intent terms for Orlando/Florida |
| Hold | **No portfolio evidence, do not target yet** | Framer, Shopify, WooCommerce, SaaS, AI integration, dental, eCommerce industry; and every country/city term except the verified HQ |

## How to complete the research (≈1 day with tool access)
1. **Volumes & difficulty:** paste the "Keyword" column into Ahrefs/Semrush (or Google Keyword Planner for volume ranges) with the United States as the primary country; add UK, Canada, Australia, Nigeria as separate columns only for terms you will actually target. Fill the CSV columns.
2. **SERP review (per P1 keyword):** note the top 10 results' page type (agency homepage, service page, directory, listicle), content depth, proof used, and whether directories (Clutch, DesignRush) dominate the results. Directories dominating means your profile there matters as much as your own page.
3. **Competitor set:** choose 8–10 agencies that repeatedly appear for P1 terms and match your size, not the global giants. Record: positioning headline, service pages, portfolio depth, review counts, referring domains, AI-answer visibility. Use the same rubric for all.
4. **Gap analysis:** list topics competitors cover that Ignixel's real work can answer *better* (e.g. direct primary care websites, vacation-rental direct booking), and decide on content from those, not from copying.
5. **Record sources and dates** so numbers can be refreshed quarterly.

## Early read from the portfolio (not from competitor data)
Ignixel's most defensible niches are visible in its own work: **Webflow for healthcare/professional services**, **WordPress with custom booking functionality for hospitality**, and **bilingual direct-booking builds**. Winning narrowly there is more realistic than competing for "web design agency" head terms, where established agencies and directories dominate. This is a hypothesis to test against SERP data.
